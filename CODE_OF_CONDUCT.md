# Code of Conduct

Partuno is a technical project built by people with different backgrounds and
levels of experience. Participants are expected to make the project a useful,
welcoming, and safe place to collaborate.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while taking impact seriously.
- Welcome questions and explain decisions with evidence.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Protect credentials, personal data, and account-specific provider data.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately exposing credentials, tokens, customer data, or provider
  account records.
- Malicious disruption, spam, impersonation, or bad-faith manipulation of the
  project.

## Enforcement

Please report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact
method in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed promptly and
kept as confidential as practical. The maintainer may edit, close, restrict,
or remove content and participation that violates this policy.

This policy applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
community channels, and project-related events.
